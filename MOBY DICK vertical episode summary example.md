**MOBY DICK**  
Or The Vertical Whale

**Chapter/Episode:** Chapter 119: THE CANDLES

**SUMMARY**  
The Pequod is struck by a Typhoon. The crew is terrified, and wants to turn out of it, but Ahab knows that if they do, they will lose Moby Dick. And so, even as the storm threatens to sink the ship, he gives a fiery speech to motivate them, framing their journey as a war against God himself.

**KEY QUOTES**  
“I see now that thy right worship is defiance\!”

**KEY THEMES**  
In this chapter, Ahab’s crazed motivations are laid bare, and he whips the crew up into an emotional maelstrom just as the Typhoon does with the Pequod.

**KEY EVENTS**  
St. Elmo’s fire forms around the mast, and lightning threatens to destroy the Pequod, but Ahab masters it, smothering the electrical glow with his own hands, seeming to disempower the storm.  His speech is the bulk of the chapter, and the power of it subdues and seduces the crew to join him in the mad quest at all costs.

**CINEMATIC IMAGES**  
The blasting storm throws the ship from wave to wave, and lightning fills the skies as Ahab rages against it in his speech, standing ridgid against the power of nature around him.
